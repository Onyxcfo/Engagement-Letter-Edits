# Onyx engagement letters — working conventions

Apply these when tailoring a client engagement letter from the HOURLY or FIXED template.

## Style
- **No em-dashes in the "What we'll do for you" proposal prose.** Use commas, parentheses, or
  colons instead (em-dashes read as AI-written). Compound hyphens (credit-card, day-to-day,
  perform-and-review) are fine. The cover tagline may keep its dash — that's Steven's layout.
- Keep client emails warm, plain, and short (no em-dashes). Reference the conversation, attach the
  letter, say we're glad to discuss/revise, and offer to send a copy for **electronic signature**.
  Lead value with team continuity + flexibility (not price).

## Before sending (final file)
- **Remove all highlighting:** strip yellow highlights AND flatten the Word form fields to plain
  text (the gray "field shading" on the dates/name). The final should have no shading and no
  leftover `[placeholders]`.
- Re-read for the old **"the the books and supporting schedules"** doubled-"the" (fixed in the
  templates, but check).
- Use **"dba"** consistently for the entity (not mixed with "d/b/a").

## Terms / scope
- **ONYX does NOT do tax work and does NOT file any tax returns.** ONYX hands the books and
  schedules to the client's tax preparer, who files. ONYX only *supports* audits (provides
  information) — it never performs or files them.
- Default **hourly**; rates $100 staff / $185 controller / $250–275 CFO. Rates guaranteed **through
  the end of the current calendar year (Dec 31)**.
- New clients get a **retainer** (amount set after reviewing the books unless told otherwise).
- Scope/fee changes are email-confirmable — no signature or formal addendum required.

## Confirm per client
- Contracting party / exact legal name (and dba), address, salutation, signer title/capacity.
- Fiscal year-end and the Initial Period.
