## Change 1 — Client Indemnification  ✅ added

**Where:** A new heading **"Client indemnification"** inserted immediately **before** the
`Finance charges and collection expenses` section (i.e., right after the `Other fees`
paragraph).

**What it does:** Shifts third-party claims back to the Client when they arise from the
Client's own data, instructions, controls, approved transactions, personnel, or legal
violations — carved back only for ONYX's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**Text used:** Verbatim from the insurance memo, with one small drafting tweak — the
parenthetical now reads
*"(collectively, the 'ONYX Parties' **and each an 'ONYX Party'**)"* so this section becomes
the single, authoritative definition of those terms (see Change 2a).

## Change 2 — Limitation of Liability  ✅ replaced

**Where:** The existing `Limitation of remedies` section.

This change has three moving parts. All three are done in the revised file:

### 2a. Defined-term cleanup (important — do not skip)
The old `Limitation of remedies` section was where `ONYX Party` was *first defined*
("...its partners, principals, directors, officers, employees, and agents (each an 'ONYX
Party')"). Your new indemnification section now appears earlier in the document **and uses a
broader list** — it adds **subcontractors and affiliates**.

To avoid two conflicting definitions of the same term, the revised letter:
- Defines `ONYX Parties` / `ONYX Party` **once**, in the new indemnification section (broad list).
- Changes the old sentence to simply: *"...it is appropriate to limit the liability of **the
  ONYX Parties**."*

> ⚠️ **Decision for you:** the broader definition (now including subcontractors and
> affiliates) flows through the **entire** agreement — the liability cap, the time
> limitation, etc. That is the more protective choice and is what your advisor's language
> implies. If you instead want subcontractors/affiliates protected by indemnity **but not**
> swept into every other limitation, tell me and I'll narrow it.

### 2b. Damages cap — replaced
- **Removed:** the old *"...any recovery on any such claims shall not exceed the fees actually
  paid under this agreement"* (capped at **all** fees ever paid).
- **Added:** the new aggregate cap = **fees paid in the 12 months immediately preceding the
  event.**

> ⚠️ **This is a real change in your favor but worth a conscious look:** on an *evergreen /
> interim* engagement, a rolling 12-month cap is usually **lower** than "all fees ever paid."
> That's stronger protection for ONYX. Just confirm you're comfortable that the cap resets on a
> trailing-12-month basis rather than accumulating over the life of the relationship.

### 2c. Consequential-damages waiver — replaced
- **Removed:** the old short *"In no event...indirect, special, incidental, consequential,
  punitive, or exemplary damages, or for loss of profits or loss of goodwill, costs, or
  attorneys' fees."*
- **Added:** the expanded waiver (adds revenue, business opportunity, anticipated savings,
  data, business-interruption losses, "even if advised of the possibility").

### What was **kept** (intentionally)
The first sentence of the old paragraph 4 — *"The Client further agrees that it will not hold
ONYX...liable for any claim...except to the extent authorized by this agreement"* — was
**retained** as the lead-in. It sets up the cap that follows. If your advisor intended to
delete it entirely, say so and I'll remove it.

The **mediation/arbitration** and **Arizona governing-law** paragraphs are untouched.

## Change 3 — Technology & Cybersecurity Risk Allocation  ✅ added

**Where:** The `Technology` section (under `Consent`).

> **Update — Steven's replacement Technology paragraph (merged, not stacked).** Steven's newer
> paragraph overlaps with the original opener (both say providers may access Client data), so it
> **replaces** the original opening paragraph rather than being added on top. The one unique
> sentence the original had that Steven's lacked — *ONYX's authority to sign vendor agreements on
> the Client's behalf and provide a copy on request* — was **preserved** as the following
> sentence so that operational authorization isn't lost. The new opener adds disclosure of
> **AI / automated data-processing tools** and a **"commercially reasonable efforts" provider-
> security standard.** Final order of the section: Steven's paragraph → vendor-signing
> authorization → the three risk-allocation paragraphs below.

**Three paragraphs added, verbatim from the memo:**
1. Client acknowledges ONYX's access and remains **solely responsible for its own system
   security/credentials.**
2. Allocates **cyber-incident risk** (phishing, social engineering, credential compromise,
   malware/ransomware, third-party provider failure) away from ONYX, except for gross
   negligence / willful misconduct.
3. ONYX may **rely on instructions/credentials reasonably believed authorized**, with no
   liability for reasonable reliance.

> 📌 **Note — these reinforce, but don't duplicate, an existing clause.** The `Other
> provisions` section already says *"If fraud is initiated by the Client's employees or other
> service providers, the Client's insurance is responsible for covering any losses."* The new
> cyber language and the new indemnity sit alongside it consistently — no conflict, and the
> overlap actually strengthens your position.
